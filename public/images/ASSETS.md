# Museum preview imagery

These are AI-generated, non-personal scenery placeholders made for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are deliberately public. Do not replace them with personal photographs; private memory files belong in the authenticated storage bucket.

| File | Purpose | Generation prompt / art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| memory-coast.png | Gallery photo placeholder | Portrait fine-art analogue travel photograph of a sunlit Mediterranean coastal path, stone steps, olive foliage, dramatic cliffs and calm blue-green sea; warm afternoon light, delicate grain, natural colours, no people, typography, logos or frame. |
| memory-meadow.png | Gallery photo placeholder | Portrait fine-art analogue landscape photograph of an olive tree on a grassy hillside, tiny wildflowers and distant hills under a warm golden sky; quiet, romantic, refined, no people, typography, logos or frame. |
| garden-twilight.png | Archived prototype; no longer loaded by the conservatory | Seamless-feeling ultra-wide twilight garden panorama; indigo sky, faint peach sunset horizon, distant rolling hills, cypress and garden silhouettes; blue hour, atmospheric and elegant, no architecture, people, typography or logos. |

Original generated PNGs were copied intact into this folder. Their paths use Vite's BASE_URL so repository subpath hosting works. Room architecture, parquet, frames, flowers and cake are real Three.js geometry/materials; no full-room concept screenshot is used as a replacement for the 3D scene.
